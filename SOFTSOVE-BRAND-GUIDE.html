6. Usage notes for graphics

  • Do not introduce a second display font. Headlines and body both use Cascadia Mono.
  • Brand color is rust red #9D2E13, not a bright orange. Hover can go to #D5350E.
  • Off-white is warm (#E9E6DE), not pure white. Avoid #FFFFFF as a page background.
  • Near-black is #16171A, not #000000.
  • Buttons: fill #9D2E13, label #FFFFFF.
  • Logos on the site: light and dark PNG versions in public/assets/img/logo-light.png and logo-dark.png.
